# Bronnikova Astro-Tarot System — Methodology

## Abstract
A synthetic tarot system built on western astrology principles. 
Each card is an equation: Planet × Element × Modality.
The deck contains 78 cards and is published as "Таро для астрологов" 
by Ves Publishing House (Russia, 2026).

## Core Principles

### 1. Planetary Ladder (Планетарная Лестница)
Instead of the Tree of Life or decan system, this deck uses a 
universal planetary-number correspondence for all four elements:

| Number | Planet   | Modality  |
|--------|----------|-----------|

| 2      | Moon     | Mutable   |
| 3      | Venus    | Cardinal  |
| 4      | Mercury  | Fixed     |
| 5      | Mars     | Cardinal  |
| 6      | Jupiter  | Fixed     |
| 7      | Saturn   | Mutable   |
| 8      | Uranus   | Cardinal  |
| 9      | Neptune  | Fixed     |
| 10     | Pluto    | Mutable   |

### 2. Elemental Spirits (Духи Стихий)
Traditional suits are replaced with elemental spirits:
- **Dragons (Драконы)** → Fire (Wands): will, action, ambition
- **Mermaids (Русалки)** → Water (Cups): feelings, intuition, relationships
- **Elves (Эльфы)** → Air (Swords): thoughts, logic, communication
- **Gnomes (Гномы)** → Earth (Pentacles): matter, resources, body

### 3. Court Cards by Modality
- **Children (Pages)** → Pure element, no zodiac sign
- **Knights** → Mutable signs (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces)
- **Queens** → Cardinal signs (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn)
- **Kings** → Fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)

### 4. Reversed Cards = Retrograde Planets
A reversed card does NOT mean "opposite meaning". It means 
retrograde motion: energy turns inward, delays, 
"requires inner work before external result".

### 6. Elemental Dignities (Classical)
Cards interact through elemental relationships:
- **Friends**: Fire+Air, Water+Earth
- **Enemies**: Fire+Water, Fire+Earth, Air+Water
- **Neutral**: Air+Earth

## How to Interpret a Card (Formula)
`Meaning = Planet's Nature × Element's Sphere × Modality's Phase`

**Example:** 5 of Dragons (5 Огня) = Mars in Aries (cardinal)
- Mars = crisis, conflict, tension
- Fire (Dragons) = sphere of will, action, ambition
- Cardinal = initiation, breakthrough
- **Result:** Battle of ambitions, active conflict in career/creative sphere
