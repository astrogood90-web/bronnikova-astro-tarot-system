# ASTROGRAMS TAROT — Астрограммы Таро

## Concept / Концепция
Astrogram (Астрограмма) is an author's tarot spread schema built around 
the themes of one astrological house: its natural sign and ruling planet 
define the logic of the spread. Each volume of the series covers one house 
and contains 26 spreads of 2–4 cards.

Principles:
- Spread theme = house theme (e.g., 10th house = Capricorn/Saturn: 
  career, status, responsibility)
- 2–4 cards per spread; position order is not important
- Works with any tarot deck; optimized for the Bronnikova Astro-Tarot System
- Every position is a precise psychological question, not a vague slot

## Published volumes / Выпуски серии
- **Vol. "12th House"** — shadow, subconscious, hidden fears, ancestral 
  scenarios, solitude, spiritual practice (26 spreads)
- **Vol. "10th House"** — career, business, status, reputation, 
  recognition, authority (26 spreads)

## Volume structure (example: 10th House, Capricorn/Saturn)
I.   Who I am becoming in my profession
II.  What is happening at my work
III. Stay, leave, or change the rules
IV.  New job and offers
V.   Own business and profit
VI.  Growth, status, reputation

Example spread titles: "Stay or leave?", "Which business suits me?", 
"How to raise my professional value?", "How to pass an important 
interview?"

## Example schema (from 12th House volume)
Spread "What is really happening?"
- Card 1: What is visible on the surface (what you already know)
- Card 2: What is happening in reality (hidden truth of the situation)

## The 4–10 house axis
The 10th house shows external realization; the 4th house is the inner 
foundation. Career built without inner support exhausts quickly. 
Astrograms of the 10th house always check this axis.
